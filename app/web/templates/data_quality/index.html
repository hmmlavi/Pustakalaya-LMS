Data Integrity Policy

Pustakalaya never fabricates missing historical dates or silently loads inconsistent legacy rows into operational tables:

  • ACCEPTED: Validated end-to-end against ISBN checksums, branch/member FKs, date ordering, and state invariants.
  • QUARANTINED: Held in staging for human librarian inspection (missing return date on closed loan, excessive renewals, unexplained fine discrepancy).
  • REJECTED: Structurally invalid (malformed ISBN checksum, inverted dates, nonexistent member/branch/copy ref, negative/overpaid fine, overlapping active loan).
